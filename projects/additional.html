A collection of smaller engineering projects spanning structural analysis, thermodynamics, mechanical systems, and product design.

Truss Analysis Program

Built a computational program to analyze two-dimensional truss structures. Given joint coordinates, member connectivity, and applied loads, the program solves for member forces using the method of joints — outputting tension/compression state and magnitude for each member. A practical application of both structural mechanics and Python programming.

Python · Structural Mechanics · Linear Algebra · Method of Joints

Balloon Propulsion Car

Designed and built a car propelled by the thrust of a deflating balloon — a classic engineering challenge that rewards careful attention to mass, rolling resistance, and nozzle geometry. Optimized the vehicle to maximize distance traveled on a single balloon inflation.

Thermodynamics · Propulsion · Lightweight Design · Fabrication

Marble Run

Designed and built a multi-stage marble run, engineering each track section to transfer momentum efficiently between stages. Focused on the geometry of curves and drops to keep the marble moving smoothly from start to finish without stalling.

Mechanical Systems · Momentum Transfer · Fabrication · Iteration

Board Game

Designed and fabricated a complete board game, handling both the mechanical production of game components and the structural design of the board itself. Used laser cutting and other fabrication tools to produce a finished, playable product.

Product Design · Laser Cutting · Fabrication · Graphic Layout

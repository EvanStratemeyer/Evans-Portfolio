Sep 2023 — Apr 2024 Personal Project

Go-Kart

Designed and built a functional go-kart capable of 30 mph, keeping total weight under 200 lbs and total cost under $1,000.

🏎   30 mph top speed · Under 200 lbs · Under $1,000 budget · Sourced from 5+ suppliers — all targets hit.

Overview

This personal project was a full-system mechanical engineering challenge: design, model, source, and build a working go-kart with real performance targets and a hard budget constraint. Led a team of two from concept to completion over the course of the school year.

Design & Modeling

Modeled the entire kart in Onshape, including the tubular steel frame, steering assembly, and drive system. Modeling all systems in a single CAD environment allowed early detection of interference and clearance issues before any material was purchased or cut.

The frame design balanced torsional rigidity against weight, while the steering geometry was laid out to provide appropriate Ackermann correction for smooth low-speed cornering.

Sourcing & Budget

Sourced components and raw materials from more than five different suppliers to hit the $1,000 budget target. This required comparing vendors, evaluating lead times, and making trade-off decisions between cost and quality on each subsystem — a real-world engineering constraint that shaped many design decisions.

Build & Testing

Fabricated and assembled the kart, iterating on fit and clearance as parts arrived. Final testing confirmed the 30 mph performance target and verified all structural connections under load.

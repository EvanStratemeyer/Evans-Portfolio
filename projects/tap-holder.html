Jan 2025 — Apr 2025 Precision Manufacturing

Machined
Tap Holder

A precision-machined tap holder manufactured from raw metal stock, holding tolerances of ±0.001″ across all operations.

⚙   All critical dimensions held to ±0.001″ — achieved through careful setup, tooling selection, and iterative measurement throughout the machining process.

Overview

This project involved manufacturing a functional tap holder from raw metal stock — a tool used to hold taps securely in a milling machine. The goal was to produce a part that meets precise design specifications, developing core machining skills across multiple operations.

Stock Preparation

Cut both round and square metal stock to rough length using horizontal and vertical band saws. Proper stock preparation is critical to reducing tool wear and ensuring consistent setups in subsequent operations.

Milling

Milled all flat surfaces and features to within one thousandth of an inch of design requirements. This required careful workholding setup, appropriate feed and speed selection, and continuous measurement with micrometers and calipers throughout the process.

Lathe Operations

Threaded and cambered components on the lathe to meet design specifications, again holding ±0.001″. Achieving this on threaded features requires proper tool geometry, consistent depth of cut, and careful lead screw engagement.

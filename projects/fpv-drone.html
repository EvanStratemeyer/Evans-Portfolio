FPV Drone
Program

Co-led a drone program, designing beginner templates and building a 5-inch FPV racing drone from scratch — including self-taught soldering.

🚁   Designed 3+ micro-drone templates for new members · Built a 5" propeller FPV racer · Self-taught soldering end-to-end.

Overview

As co-leader of the drone program, the goal was two-fold: lower the barrier to entry for new members by creating reusable build templates, and personally push the limits by constructing a high-performance FPV racing drone from scratch.

Micro-Drone Templates

Designed more than three micro-drone frame and component templates optimized for new builders. These templates standardized part selection, simplified wiring layouts, and reduced build time — making it easier for members with no prior experience to complete their first drone and get racing quickly.

5-Inch FPV Racing Drone

Researched, selected, and built a full 5-inch propeller FPV (First Person View) racing drone. This class of drone is among the most capable and complex in the hobby — requiring careful balancing of motor KV, ESC current ratings, battery C-rating, and frame rigidity.

Taught myself to solder in order to complete the build, working through flight controller wiring, ESC connections, motor leads, and video transmitter integration. All electronics were soldered and heat-shrunk by hand.

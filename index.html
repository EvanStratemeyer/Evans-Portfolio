About Me

I’m a mechanical engineering student at Boston University who loves designing, building, and iterating—especially when the work spans CAD, fabrication, and real-world testing.

I’m most energized by projects where the constraints are real: tight tolerances, weight budgets, reliability, and clean integration between parts and systems. Lately, that’s meant aircraft subsystems, precision machining, and hands-on builds that push my craftsmanship.

I’m actively looking for internships and hands-on engineering opportunities where I can contribute quickly and learn even faster.
